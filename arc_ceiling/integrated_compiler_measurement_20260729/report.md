## What was integrated

One package now contains:

- the protected complete Kerdock seed-3 asset;
- the current depth-5 partial-tree Strassen-Winograd multiplication;
- a shared immutable, backend-generic symbolic suffix compiler;
- frozen two-layer `c=8, rare=1` mode;
- fixed three-layer `c=8, rare=8`, middle correction `0`, final correction `0.875`, and `0.995` full-suffix guard;
- adaptive depths 2–6 with the shared 2,064-row pilot, rare threshold `1`, minimum predicted arithmetic selection, and `0.995` guard;
- a clean-subprocess official runner and paired aggregator.

The candidate and baseline paths share the same first-layer design and partial-tree prefix propagation. Candidate approximation error is computed against the baseline prediction generated by the same package and same network, preventing cross-baseline projection comparisons.

## Validation completed here

Six unit tests pass:

1. partial-tree Winograd agrees with dense multiplication;
2. an all-kink compiler exactly recovers the full suffix mean;
3. classification using every row exactly recovers the full suffix mean;
4. the minimum-saving guard falls back to the exact suffix;
5. adaptive selection chooses only legal depths and returns finite predictions;
6. stable-on, stable-off, and kink partitions cover every coordinate.

A reduced width-32 structural smoke ran all four modes on eight synthetic networks. It validates control flow, timing fields, fallbacks, and approximation-error reporting only. Its numerical errors and timings are not evidence about the width-256 competition distribution.

## Gate decision

The adoption gate is **not cleared** because no current-package official Mini-100 raw MSE, exact FlopScope cost, subprocess residual wall time, effective compute, memory, or tails were measured.

**Preserve the unchanged production baseline.** The scoped compiler decision is:

- keep fixed three-layer as the simplest first candidate for the real paired run;
- keep adaptive 2–6 as the direct challenger whose small projected advantage may disappear under selection and symbolic overhead;
- keep frozen two-layer as the validated fallback;
- adopt none until `results/official_paired/summary.json` exists and clears every gate.

This is an infrastructure block, not a scientific rejection of suffix compilation.
