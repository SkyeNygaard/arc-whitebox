# WHestBench Paper A — reviewer questions

The request is intentionally bounded. A reviewer need not audit the complete experiment ledger.

## Theorem and scope

1. Is the static estimator class stated naturally and without hidden assumptions?
2. Does the nonnegative result support “within 0.0233242% of optimum,” given the stated interval-verification dependency?
3. Is the arbitrary-signed theorem correctly separated from the stronger nonnegative theorem?
4. Is it correct to exclude the earlier arbitrary-total-mass corollary until its standalone witness is recovered, and should the mass-one restriction be more prominent?
5. Are the finite-width, adaptive, nonlinear, and network-dependent escape classes sufficiently prominent?

## Proof architecture

6. Is the all-degree degree-five auxiliary optimization adequately justified by the Hermite contact conditions, exact quadrature, and reduced-cost sign argument?
7. Is the positive-index lemma and its transfer through atomic inertia correct?
8. Is the sign-count hierarchy a useful corollary or better placed in an appendix?
9. Is the Schur–Horn shared-Gram sharpness theorem stated at the right generality?
10. Is the equality-to-zero-code argument correct under the full-row-rank condition?
11. Does the consecutive-Gegenbauer common-zero obstruction establish only strict nonattainment, as claimed?
12. Is the coalescing-cancellation example sufficient to explain why strictness does not give a uniform unrestricted signed gap?

## Computer-assisted evidence

13. Which numerical interval claims must be independently reconstructed before a preprint?
14. Is exact-rational replay of the witness sufficient once the kernel intervals are independently certified?
15. What should be placed in the main paper versus a verification appendix or repository?

## Positioning

17. Which related work or prior near-optimality theorem is missing?
18. Which venue or workshop is the best initial target?
