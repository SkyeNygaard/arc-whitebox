# Open problems

## Highest-value verification problems

1. **Independent interval reconstruction.** Rebuild the load-bearing depth-32 Gegenbauer coefficient endpoints in Arb, FLINT, MPFR, or another directed stack.
2. **Recover the exact baseline.** Locate and authenticate `whestbench_final_129basis_20260730.tar.gz` and the official Mini-100 JSON.
3. **Reproduce the mixture bundle.** Recover scripts/arrays for the K ladder, Taylor recentering, and rank sweep.

## Mathematical problems after the static theorem

1. **Actual width-256 arbitrary-node floor.** Transfer a strong signed/nonnegative certificate to the finite-width ensemble without invalid coefficient monotonicity assumptions.
2. **Quantitative realizability gap.** Strengthen the signed floor using sphere-ideal, product-linearization, Hankel/catalecticant, multiplication-operator, or bounded-total-variation constraints.
3. **Sparse signed construction.** Determine whether any realizable rule approaches the 1.06717 signed ceiling with very few negative-weight support entries, and characterize the required total variation and conditioning.
4. **Adaptive nonlinear estimator.** Use realized weights or complete-basis outputs in a way that is provably outside the static class and wins after cost.
5. **Analytic residual anchor.** Find an exactly integrable network-dependent surrogate with strong complete-block residual shrinkage.
6. **Structured covariance diagonal.** Find a genuinely subcubic identity for the recurring covariance contractions without reconstructing the full state.
7. **Gate-conditioned boundary current.** Derive an all-ancestor phase-bearing identity not killed by complete-basis quadratic Parseval.
