# Release gates

## Gate A — public external-review release

Status: **pass after strict clean-checkout verification**.

Requirements:

- all public constants use replayed witnesses;
- exact scope appears in abstract, README, forum post, and reviewer overview;
- manifest covers every file and all hashes match;
- core verification passes from a fresh extraction;
- missing empirical artifacts are disclosed;
- historical/superseded theory reports are labeled.

## Gate B — formal Paper A preprint

Status: **conditional**.

Before calling the result externally verified:

- obtain a named mathematical review;
- independently reconstruct the inherited directed kernel interval stack;
- publish the frozen archive digest outside the archive;
- run public multi-platform CI;
- complete human related-work review.

Paper A may be circulated earlier if explicitly labeled a computer-assisted external-review draft with inherited interval inputs.

## Gate C — formal Paper B empirical paper

Status: **not passed**.

Requirements:

- recover or rerun the principal empirical bundles;
- include per-network rows, scripts, arrays, metric definitions, seeds, and environment;
- authenticate the exact shipping estimator package and official result JSON;
- preserve grouped validation and protected-data governance.

Until then, Paper B should be released as an open technical report and research ledger.

## Gate D — claims that remain prohibited

- Kerdock is exactly globally optimal.
- The theorem covers finite-width networks.
- The theorem covers adaptive or nonlinear estimators.
- Do not claim that the signed theorem is an equal-cost theorem.
- The factor cap `1.067167...` means a `6.7167%` risk reduction.
- The unrecovered reoptimized T70 fraction is the audited theorem.
- Negative-weight support count bounds total negative mass.
- Strict nonattainment of the older abstract floor automatically transfers to the strengthened floor.
