# Empirical closure statements and reopening gates

## Layer-31 anchors

**Closed:** all currently tested anchor families and the frozen T4 policy.  
**Not closed:** every possible independent absolute-phase observable.

Reopen only with:

- immutable grouped base-network/rotation rows;
- independent high-quality target/reference streams;
- frozen direction families and perturbation conventions;
- downstream-weighted `eta_J` and exact nonlinear replay MSE;
- wins, p90, worst, grouped uncertainty, and complete compute accounting;
- no protected-cohort tuning.

## Scalar learning

**Closed:** tested archived Path-2 grouped linear and small nonlinear models as evidence of useful network-specific scale variation.  
**Removed:** M152.

Reopen only with:

- genuinely independent base-network corpus;
- exact target equation and reliable labels;
- frozen legal features and extraction code;
- grouped train/calibration/test chronology;
- safe constant, development-optimal constant, and same-mean constant baselines;
- row predictions, final ratios, tails, uncertainty, and hashes.

## Harmonic controls

**Closed exactly:** named degree-at-most-five radialized polynomial classes, polynomial Stein fields through component degree four, and the stated bias-free one-hidden-layer ReLU Stein class.  
**Closed empirically:** the selected small degree-6+8 dictionary campaign.  
**Open:** richer high-degree, nonpolynomial, biased, deep, nonhomogeneous, and network-adaptive controls.

Reopen only after an exact legal expectation, an oracle ceiling at deployable scale, frozen selection, and independent grouped validation.

## Arbitrary signed rules

**Closed:** signed reweighting in the fixed Kerdock-line universe.  
**Open:** arbitrary signed nodes.

Reopen with either:

- a stronger geometry-aware global certificate; or
- an explicit certified construction with complete compute and tail performance.

## Finite-width transfer

The main theorem is limiting-kernel. Reopen the finite-width bridge with a preregistered width-256 grouped study or a rigorous analytic finite-width correction. Do not infer finite-width optimality from the limiting theorem.

## Operational program-level conclusion

The correct stopping statement is not universal impossibility. It is:

> The currently active branches are closed within their tested information classes and frozen protocols. A continuation requires a qualitatively new observable, theorem class, support geometry, or independently validated algorithm—not another retuning of a closed family.
