# Counterexamples and scope boundaries — Agent 4 / T16

## Low dimension

The recovered V3 dual audit has positive higher-degree reduced costs at `d=4` (including degrees 8, 9, and 15). Therefore the target theorem is not a generic consequence of degree-5 exactness or of using a three-point dual measure.

## What the theorem does not imply

The theorem does not prove:

- optimality for arbitrary signed node weights;
- optimality for network-dependent or adaptive rules;
- finite-width width-256 optimality;
- optimality of nonlinear estimators;
- that every alternative auxiliary formulation has the same dual measure.

## Primal-attainment boundary

Strict negativity of every unused reduced cost proves all-degree dual feasibility of the exact three-node moment measure. Exact all-degree LP optimality for a named degree-5 primal polynomial additionally requires exact primal feasibility and equality of primal and dual objectives. A safely downward-shifted rational minorant is feasible but does not attain contact exactly.
