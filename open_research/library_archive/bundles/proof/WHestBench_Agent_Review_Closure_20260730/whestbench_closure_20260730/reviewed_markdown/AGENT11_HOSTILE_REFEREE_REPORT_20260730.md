# Agent 11 — Final Hostile Referee Report

**Project:** ARC White-Box Estimation Challenge 2026 / WHestBench  
**Date:** 2026-07-30  
**Materials reviewed:** Agent 1–8 Markdown reports and their cited machine-readable certificates available in the shared Library  
**Recommendation:** **REJECT IN CURRENT FORM; ENCOURAGE RESUBMISSION AFTER MAJOR RECONSTRUCTION**

## 1. Executive verdict

The reviewed package contains a genuinely strong, publishable mathematical core:

1. a scoped computer-assisted near-optimality theorem for static, network-independent, nonnegative-weight cubature under the dimension-256, depth-32 infinite-width ReLU kernel;
2. an exact restricted signed-weight theorem inside the fixed antipodal Kerdock-line universe;
3. a completed all-degree reduced-cost negativity theorem;
4. useful correction-risk, replacement, common-bias, and ReLU-crossing lemmas; and
5. a disciplined taxonomy of exact annihilation results and frozen empirical failures.

However, the broader narrative suggested by the research program is not supported. The reports themselves refute or leave open every universal reading of the following claims:

- “no statistical path exists”;
- the three obstruction faces are one theorem;
- signed weights are globally closed;
- adaptive or nonlinear white-box estimators are closed;
- a universal layer-31 relative-error threshold is approximately `5e-4`;
- scalar learning is impossible;
- analytically integrable controls are necessarily low degree; or
- infinite-width kernel attribution directly describes width-256 networks.

Two prominent empirical objects, M146 and M152, are not independently reproducible from the shared artifacts. M152 must be removed from the evidentiary record. M146 may be mentioned only as a provisional reported curve whose arithmetic is internally consistent; it cannot support a universal precision threshold or a central empirical claim.

The result should therefore be reconstructed as either:

- a narrow theorem paper centered on T22, T27, and the all-degree reduced-cost result; or
- a theorem paper plus a separate empirical falsification-map article that carefully labels each tested information class and avoids universal impossibility rhetoric.

I would not accept an integrated manuscript until the fatal issues below are resolved.

## 3. Fatal issues

### F1. The proposed universal negative thesis is contradicted by the review package

The strongest defensible conclusion is not that no statistical correction exists. It is:

> No active branch in the tested information classes clears a credible continuation gate under the present deadline, evidence, and resource constraints.

Agent 3 gives explicit nonlinear and network-dependent constructions showing why a restricted static linear theorem cannot imply adaptive or nonlinear impossibility. Agent 5 finds that the unrestricted signed-weight stability bound permits meaningful gains with extremely small negative mass. Agents 6 and 7 explicitly leave genuinely new absolute-phase observables and scalar predictors mathematically open. Agent 8 constructs an analytically integrable control with nonzero content at every even harmonic degree.

Any title, abstract, conclusion, or figure implying universal “no statistical path,” universal analytic-control impossibility, or a single no-free-lunch theorem is grounds for rejection.

### F2. M152 is absent and must be deleted as evidence

The 1,100-network corpus, scalar target, feature definitions, grouped split, script, row predictions, and hashes cannot be located. Reported correlations, cross-validation results, and complete estimator ratios are therefore unverified. The substitute Path-2 audit is useful but answers a different, narrower question.

M152 may appear only in a reproducibility-failure appendix as an example of a claim removed during audit. It must not appear in the abstract, results table, conclusion, or evidentiary narrative.

### F3. M146 cannot support the paper’s advertised precision barrier

The original 60-network artifact package, identifiers, perturbation directions, normalization, seeds, and replay outputs are missing. The reported points fit a quadratic curve almost perfectly, but that establishes only arithmetic consistency. Agent 6 demonstrates that equal-norm break-even precision can vary by orders of magnitude with downstream direction, and that kink-focused directions can produce substantial nonlinear remainder.

The scalar statement “the anchor must be accurate to approximately `5e-4`” is not invariant and is false as a universal claim. The admissible criterion is downstream weighted and replay specific. Until M146 is reconstructed, its numerical headlines must be labeled provisional and excluded from central claims.

### F4. The signed-weight loophole remains open globally

T27 closes arbitrary real weights only on symmetrized lines in the fixed Kerdock universe. Proposition 5 gives a general signed-measure lower bound, but the numerical exclusion curve is too weak to rule out competition-relevant gains: even a 10% Kerdock-relative gain is compatible with negative mass on the order of `7.1e-7`.

The paper may claim:

> Signed reweighting does not help inside the fixed Kerdock-line universe, and the global positive certificate is quantitatively stable to sufficiently tiny negative mass.

It may not claim that signed cubature is closed.

### F5. Finite-width relevance is unestablished

The principal cubature theorems concern an infinite-width depth-32 kernel at dimension 256. The challenge networks have finite width 256. The reports repeatedly warn that limiting-kernel harmonic shares, oracle ceilings, and static ensemble-risk statements are not finite-width measurements or impossibility theorems.

A paper aimed at the actual challenge must either provide a preregistered finite-width transfer study with uncertainty and grouped network sampling, or state plainly that the theorem characterizes a limiting model and that its finite-network relevance is empirical and incomplete.

### F6. T16 must not be upgraded beyond what is proved

The reviewed certificate supports strict negativity of every unused reduced cost, using exact arithmetic through degree 14,658 and an analytic tail thereafter. That does not by itself establish exact primal-dual equality for the named degree-5 minorant. The paper must either supply an exact primal feasibility, attainment, and complementarity certificate, or state only the reduced-cost theorem.

Additionally, the original governance plan called for independent 4A and 4B attempts frozen before reconciliation. I found the resulting certificate and decision but did not locate a distinct frozen 4B report using a genuinely different arithmetic stack. This is a reproducibility and independence gap, not a demonstrated mathematical error.

### F7. The computer-assisted proof release is not publication-ready yet

Agent 1 found a stale machine-readable artifact with an invalid two-sided interpretation. Agent 2 found imprecise “immutable manifest” language, a 31-versus-32 file-count discrepancy, untracked intermediate chunks, and an insufficiently pinned platform matrix.

Before publication, the authors must remove or quarantine the stale artifact, publish one canonical archive, externally anchor its digest, identify every proof-critical file, document intermediate regeneration, and run the verifier in a pinned multi-platform CI environment. Until then, the theorem can be described as mathematically verified by the audit but the release itself is not final.

### F8. Final evidence and public-accounting audits are missing from this review set

The requested Agent 11 remit includes public-accounting claims, contamination chronology, and complete traceability of headline numbers. Agents 1–8 do not constitute the planned Agent 9 evidence audit or an assembled Agent 10 manuscript. I therefore cannot certify public high-row accounting, complete selection chronology, novelty framing, or that every manuscript number is linked to raw rows and scripts.

Acceptance requires the evidence-auditor register and the actual assembled manuscript, not merely the component reports.

## 4. Major issues

### M1. Novelty is not yet demonstrated

The theorem package appears technically substantial, but novelty cannot be assessed from internal reports alone. The manuscript needs a serious related-work comparison covering spherical designs and energy bounds, Delsarte linear programming, MUB/Kerdock cubature, kernel quadrature, random-feature and neural-network Gaussian-process limits, adaptive cubature, control variates, and white-box neural expectation estimation.

The contribution should be stated as a new certificate for a specific deep-ReLU kernel and node budget, not as discovery of the general limitations of neural integration.

### M2. The paper needs a theorem-scope matrix at the front

Each principal claim should explicitly list:

- dimension and depth;
- finite- versus infinite-width setting;
- deterministic versus randomized rule;
- independence from the realized network;
- support universe;
- antipodal symmetrization;
- node or line budget;
- real versus nonnegative weights;
- linear versus nonlinear estimator; and
- whether the conclusion is exact, computer-assisted, oracle, frozen empirical, or exploratory.

Without this matrix, scope leakage is nearly inevitable.

### M3. Oracle diagnostics are repeatedly at risk of overinterpretation

Exact-anchor gain, per-network optimal coefficients, harmonic-component removal, and per-rotation selection are useful mechanism diagnostics. They are not deployable estimators and do not establish observability. Every oracle table and plot should be visually separated and labeled “ORACLE DIAGNOSTIC.” No oracle number should be used as the numerator of a deployable performance claim without a legal estimator and frozen evaluation.

### M4. Empirical contamination and effective sample size require a unified audit

Rotations of the same base network are not independent. Row-wise cross-validation can be materially optimistic. Mini-100 and other exposed panels must be labeled development-only. Every empirical claim needs base-network grouping, exact selection chronology, target/reference streams, noise correction, and confidence intervals at the group level.

The current package contains some exemplary local audits but not one global, manuscript-level contamination table.

### M5. Target noise weakens learning conclusions

In the archived Path-2 holdout, reference noise is a substantial fraction of baseline MSE for many rows. This does not invalidate the reproduced comparisons, but it limits conclusions about weak predictors and makes “negative R²” an ambiguous mixture of model failure and label reliability. The paper should quantify a noise ceiling or attenuation bound before using these experiments as evidence about learnability.

### M6. The harmonic section must remain a taxonomy

Exact annihilation holds for specified low-degree radialized polynomials, polynomial Stein fields through a stated component degree, and a particular bias-free one-hidden-layer ReLU Stein class. The frozen degree-6+8 dictionary failed, while broader high-degree analytic controls remain open.

These are complementary results with different assumptions and evidence levels. They cannot be merged into one theorem or summarized as “analytic controls vanish.”

### M7. AI-generated proofs and experiments require independent authorship controls

The Library metadata marks the reviewed reports as model generated. That does not make their mathematics invalid, but it means the manuscript cannot treat agreement among agents as independent expert verification by itself. The final release should disclose AI assistance, identify which derivations and code were generated or modified by models, and require named human authors to inspect every theorem statement, proof-critical script, certificate, and empirical table.

At minimum, one independent human mathematical reviewer should sign off on T22/T27/T16, and one independent human reproducibility reviewer should rerun the release from a clean environment.

### M8. The integrated paper is trying to carry too many logical layers

The strongest presentation is likely two artifacts:

1. **Theorem paper:** kernel-risk identity, T22 certificate, T27 restricted support theorem, T16 reduced-cost theorem, signed-mass stability lemma, exact scope and trust base.
2. **Empirical falsification article:** anchor theory, failed legal estimators, harmonic controls, learning, coresets, implementation economics, contamination controls, and explicit reopening conditions.

Combining them is possible only with severe discipline. Otherwise the empirical failures will blur theorem scope and weaken the main mathematical contribution.

## 6. Presentation issues

### P1. Title and abstract

The title should foreground the certified static boundary, not universal impossibility. A defensible formulation is:

> **A Certified Boundary for Static Deep-ReLU Cubature, with Scoped Obstructions to Cheap White-Box Correction**

The abstract must state the exclusions—finite width, adaptive supports or weights, nonlinear estimators, and unrestricted signed nodes—in the same paragraph as the principal theorem.

### P2. Results hierarchy

Use four visibly distinct labels throughout:

- **PROVED / COMPUTER-ASSISTED CERTIFIED**;
- **PROVED UNDER AN EXPLICIT MODEL**;
- **FROZEN EMPIRICAL**;
- **ORACLE DIAGNOSTIC / EXPLORATORY**.

Do not place them in a single undifferentiated “main results” table.

### P3. Counterexamples should be included, not hidden in limitations

The nonlinear one-neuron estimator, the network-dependent exact-node construction, and the analytic Poisson-kernel control are excellent scope guards. Including them will increase credibility and prevent readers from making precisely the overextensions the current narrative invites.

### P4. Negative results need normalized denominators

For each failed method, state the exact information class, feature family, cohort, selection chronology, compute cost, strongest constant baseline, and reopening condition. Avoid long catalogs of abandoned ideas without a common comparison structure.

## 10. Final decision

**REJECT IN CURRENT FORM.**

This is not a rejection of the core mathematics. The static positive-weight certificate, restricted Kerdock-line theorem, and all-degree reduced-cost result are potentially strong contributions. The rejection is of the attempted synthesis into a broad impossibility narrative. The agents’ own hostile audits show that the strongest honest paper is narrower, more precise, and more credible than the current thesis.
