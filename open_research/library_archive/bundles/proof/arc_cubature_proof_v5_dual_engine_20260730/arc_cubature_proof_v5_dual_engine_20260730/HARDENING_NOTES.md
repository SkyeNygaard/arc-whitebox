# V5 strengthened release notes

This release does not broaden the theorem's estimator class. It strengthens the evidence and reduces the software/construction trust base.

## New evidence

1. **Explicit Kerdock/MUB incidence certificate.** A clean Python-integer implementation constructs all 128 chirps, checks the GF(128) arithmetic, exact Walsh orthogonality, all 8,128 unordered chirp-basis pairs, and the exact fixed-node multiplicities. The independently reconstructed chirps match the archived production asset exactly.
2. **Implementation-diverse decimal checks.** The standard-library pure-Python `_pydecimal` implementation regenerates the assembled interval certificate, sign audit, multiplicity proof, and spherical kernel mean byte-for-byte. It also independently replays the exact Delsarte, energy, MSE, and one-sided ratio arithmetic from the certified interval inputs.
3. **Non-hard-coded sign audit.** A separate verifier derives the critical-point types and connecting derivative signs from certificate fields rather than embedding the intended sign pattern.
4. **Complete release manifest.** `FULL_ARTIFACT_MANIFEST.sha256` covers every release file except itself, including all 23 curvature chunks and the strengthening evidence. `verify_full_artifact_manifest.py` rejects missing, modified, or unexpected files.

## Corrections to earlier wording

- The original `PROOF_AUDIT.md` says 31 deterministic files; `PROOF_MANIFEST.sha256` actually contains 32 entries.
- “Immutable manifest” should be read as fixed during verification. The separately distributed archive SHA-256 binds the whole release; it is not a digital signature or third-party timestamp.

## Remaining limitations

- The complete 1,079-interval curvature mesh was regenerated with the original CPython C-decimal implementation. Three representative difficult one-interval chunks, including indices 550, 605, and 606, were regenerated byte-for-byte with pure `_pydecimal`; a full pure-`_pydecimal` mesh regeneration was not completed.
- Cross-operating-system and cross-Python-version reproduction is still pending.
- This remains a computer-assisted proof, not a Lean/Coq/Isabelle formalization.
- The theorem still excludes signed weights, network-adaptive rules, pilot adaptation, nonlinear estimators, network-dependent residual methods, and finite-width objectives.
