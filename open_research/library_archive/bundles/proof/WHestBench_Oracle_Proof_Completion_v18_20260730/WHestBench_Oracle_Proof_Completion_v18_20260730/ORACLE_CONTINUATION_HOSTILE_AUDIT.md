# Oracle continuation — hostile audit and canonical disposition

**Date:** 2026-07-30  
**Input state:** canonical v17 project closeout, OracleContinuation package, Agent3 cascade package, BestTheoremTargets package, and the earlier Oracle proof-progress memo.  
**Disposition:** the continuation materially strengthens the proof program, but several headline interpretations require narrowing. No protected cohort was opened.

## Executive judgment

The continuation produced four durable advances:

1. authenticated checkpoint-level Oracle arrays now support a pooled cross-checkpoint diagnostic;
2. the exact five-source and scalar-source experiments close their frozen source/feature/policy classes;
3. a global rank obstruction gives a nonzero lower bound for every static arbitrary-signed mass-one rule at the target node budget;
4. exact symmetry, information, and quotient theorems now state what would be required to turn the empirical phase pattern into a real impossibility result.

The continuation does **not** establish that the finite-width network error is intrinsically high-rank within each network, that the full legal transcript has little phase information, or that adaptive/nonlinear estimation is impossible.

## 2. Cross-checkpoint Oracle coherence

The package reports pooled signed repair increments at checkpoints 7, 15, 23, 27, 29 and 30. Maximum absolute off-diagonal cosines are:

| Split | Maximum absolute pooled cosine | Effective rank of normalized pooled Gram | Cumulative energy / sum increment energy |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Development | 0.302 | 3.824 | 0.937 |
| Validation | 0.146 | 3.803 | 0.971 |
| Confirmation | 0.187 | 4.287 | 1.078 |

The last two increments contribute only 7.25%, 8.23% and 9.84% of the summed increment energy on the three splits.

### What is established

- The **pooled concatenated increments** are not one common direction with monotonically increasing amplitude.
- Middle checkpoints already expose large repairability.
- Aggregate cross terms are modest relative to summed increment energy.
- A one-scalar checkpoint story is an inadequate description of the pooled campaign.

### What is not established

Low pooled cosine can arise entirely from cross-case sign cancellation. For two scalar cases, the pooled vectors

\[
u_1=(1,1),\qquad u_2=(1,-1)
\]

have zero cosine even though each case has one-dimensional output and perfectly collinear increments. Therefore the pooled result does not prove within-network rank, independent causal sources, or approximately orthogonal components for a typical network.

### Correct status

**REPLICATED POOLED HETEROGENEITY DIAGNOSTIC.**  
The missing stronger object is a grouped distribution of per-network increment Gram matrices or a common transported-mode decomposition.

## 5. New theorem audit

### T42 — positive-definite optimized residual

The coefficientwise proof is coherent. The strengthened order-47 directed interval jet produces strict margins in degrees 0 through 5. Publication still requires an independent interval stack and human review.

### T43 — arbitrary-signed-node rank floor

The abstract matrix-rank theorem is conventional and correct. The original exploratory certificate displayed only even active degrees in its minimum. The squared reproducing kernel also has positive coefficients in degrees 1, 3 and 5. This package checks all active degrees 1 through 6; degree 6 still binds. The strengthened candidate bound is

\[
R_{K_{32}}(Q)\ge 1.7017556669835916\times10^{-8},
\]

or at least 6.9925767% of complete-Kerdock MSE. It permits at most approximately 14.30088x improvement. This is a meaningful global floor, but not signed near-optimality.

### T47 — weighted harmonic rank floor

A further exact generalization replaces the unweighted harmonic covariance by a weighted covariance. The best frozen degree-15 rational certificate checks every active degree 1 through 30 and gives

\[
R_{K_{32}}(Q)\ge1.2294295437956858\times10^{-7}
=0.505177125470747\,R_{\mathrm{Kerdock}}.
\]

Thus every static, network-independent, arbitrary-signed mass-one rule at the target node budget can improve by at most 1.979503722x. This supersedes T43 numerically while retaining the same scope guards. It is the strongest new proof result in this continuation and still requires an independent interval stack.

### T44 — information bounds

The binary phase and Fano inequalities are exact. They become WHestBench results only after a valid mutual-information or KL upper bound for an actual legal transcript. Failed predictors do not supply such a bound.

### T45 — symmetry-defect bound

The exact identity is valid and useful because it supports approximate, measurable symmetry rather than an all-or-nothing assertion. Its WHestBench application remains conditional on a real measure-preserving transformation.

### T46 — gauge obstruction

This is the cleanest exact application to the current phase work. Orientation-blind quotient features cannot determine representation-dependent signed coefficients. The theorem closes a coefficient-architecture mistake, not the physical estimator class. A legal escape must expose an orientation-odd canonical anchor.

The new theorems close static signed rules only partially and close orientation-blind coefficient representations exactly; they do not rule out adaptive nonlinear estimators or new orientation-sensitive runtime information.
