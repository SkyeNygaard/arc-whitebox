# Proposed collision-free ledger additions — beyond v21

## T61 — Simultaneous abstract block-trace sharpness

**Evidence:** exact linear algebra plus numerical unit test.  
**Claim:** one shared abstract block matrix simultaneously attains the rank/block-trace floor for every nonnegative harmonic profile after diagonal rescaling.  
**Verdict:** all joint relaxations using only shared rank and individual block traces are exhausted.  
**Exclusions:** spherical evaluation realizability, multiplication identities, atomic moment constraints.

## T62 — Strict nonattainment of the degree-280 signed certificate

**Evidence:** exact certificate metadata, atomic equality theorem, exact polynomial gcd/resultant/Bézout identity.  
**Claim:** no actual mass-one atomic spherical rule attains the released `0.9370459569114724 R_K` floor.  
**Verdict:** the abstract boundary is not realizable.  
**Exclusions:** no uniform numerical improvement over unrestricted signed rules follows without compactness/conditioning.

## T63 — Bounded-total-variation gap and blow-up

**Evidence:** compactness plus T62.  
**Claim:** for every finite TV bound V there exists epsilon(V)>0 above the certificate floor; any near-floor sequence must have TV diverging.  
**Verdict:** stable bounded signed weights cannot approach the abstract boundary.  
**Exclusions:** epsilon(V) is nonconstructive and not competition-sized.

## T64 — Conditioned two-profile realizability stability

**Evidence:** exact matrix inequality and exact common-zero margin.  
**Claim:** if active weights and evaluation singular values are bounded below, the two degree-3/4 profile excesses satisfy a positive quantitative inequality.  
**Verdict:** near equality forces vanishing weights or ill-conditioned/coalescing evaluation geometry.  
**Exclusions:** current explicit constant is conservative.

## T65 — Exact Gaussian ReLU gate-crossing moments

**Evidence:** exact bivariate Gaussian integration; direct quadrature and Monte Carlo verification.  
**Claim:** conditional first and second moments of the ReLU crossing remainder are explicit functions of activation angle.  
**Verdict:** use in prefix-measurable Gaussian suffixes instead of a generic density bound.  
**Exclusions:** current/future-row-dependent perturbations and complete deep cross-term control.

## T66 — Finite-width coefficient-monotonicity no-go

**Evidence:** exact width-one ReLU construction and symbolic series.  
**Claim:** finite-width kernel coefficients need not dominate infinite-width coefficients.  
**Verdict:** degree-128 transfer needs architecture-specific quantitative coefficient bounds.  
**Exclusions:** does not determine the sign of width-256 coefficient corrections.

## T67 — Conditional A51 full-interface equivalence

**Evidence:** exact block inversion.  
**Claim:** with distinct selected coordinates and invertible V_{I^c}, `(Vd,Ed)` determines the full center defect.  
**Verdict:** generic full-128 interface estimation is not a dimensional reduction.  
**Exclusions:** actual frozen V rank remains unaudited because the direction arrays were not directly retained.
