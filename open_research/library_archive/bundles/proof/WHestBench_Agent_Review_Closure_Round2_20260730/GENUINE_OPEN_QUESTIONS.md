# Genuine open questions after round 2

## 1. Official implementation choice

**Dependency:** production versus A42 versus A43, with A43-delta64 and basis-count arms used only for economics calibration.  
**Why open:** the official subprocess wall-time, FlopScope, and score environment is unavailable here.  
**Closure gate:** run the already frozen suite without tuning, verify output equality, and choose by complete effective cost.

## 2. T22/T23 public release completion

**Dependency:** external archive authentication and a final independent release run.  
**Why open:** external digest anchoring, full 1,421-box regeneration, multi-platform CI, and named sign-off remain.  
**Closure gate:** one public canonical archive whose commands pass from a clean checkout and whose digest is externally anchored.

## 3. T30 hostile human review

**Dependency:** named mathematical review of the computer-assisted primal-dual closure.  
**Why open:** the sixth-derivative proof, Faà di Bruno/Bell inequalities, Hermite remainder, coefficient positivity, and uniqueness should not rely only on model-generated audits.  
**Closure gate:** reviewer reruns the exact Python and independent C++ paths and signs the precise theorem scope.

## 4. Arbitrary-node finite-width T22

**Dependency:** a finite-width global certificate, not merely fixed-support symmetry.  
**Why open:** T38 closes the fixed MUB-line universe only. Uniform kernel perturbation would need a bound many orders of magnitude smaller than currently available.  
**Closure gate:** a finite-width Delsarte/SDP certificate or a structure-aware harmonic/kernel bound strong enough to preserve the certified sandwich.

## 5. Arbitrary-node signed finite-width rules

**Dependency:** geometry-aware signed analysis.  
**Why open:** T38 permits arbitrary signs only on the fixed MUB-line universe; the general negative-mass lemma is quantitatively weak.  
**Closure gate:** a substantially stronger signed certificate or a stable explicit improving construction.

## 6. Actual low-cost information symmetry

**Dependency:** connect T39 to a real legal observation map.  
**Why open:** no exact measure-preserving group action has been proved to leave the actual low-cost observables invariant while removing the relevant signed error component.  
**Closure gate:** state the observation sigma-field and prove the symmetry, or exhibit and validate a group-noninvariant signed component.

## 7. Constructive live-degree residual surrogate

**Dependency:** a cheap legal exactly integrable surrogate affecting harmonic degrees at least six.  
**Why open:** T40 supplies the residual-kernel arithmetic and recertification route, not the surrogate itself.  
**Closure gate:** freeze a common transformation, derive its residual kernel, recertify the rule, and pass raw MSE, tails, and complete-cost gates.

## 8. M146 and M152 provenance

**Dependency:** original immutable packages.  
**Why open:** the rows, target definitions, split chronologies, directions/features, predictions, code, and hashes remain absent.  
**Closure gate:** recover the originals exactly. A new experiment with a new specification is not a reproduction; absent recovery, exclusion is permanent.
