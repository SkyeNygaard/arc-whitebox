# Counterexamples and adversarial cases

## No counterexample to Proposition 5

No violation was found, and the symbolic proof is complete under the assumptions.

## Counterexample to an overly broad interpretation

The beta threshold is **necessary, not sufficient**. Inside the complete Kerdock-line universe, the stronger exact objective proves that no signed weighting improves the optimum, even though Proposition 5 permits improvements once beta exceeds a tiny threshold.

## Representation pathology

If duplicate support points are allowed as separate entries, one may add weights `+a` and `-a` at the same node. The estimator is unchanged while beta increases by `a`. Therefore beta must be defined for the consolidated signed measure (Jordan decomposition), not for an arbitrary list representation.

## Scope escape

Proposition 5 does not exclude:

* nodes outside the Kerdock-line universe;
* network-dependent signed measures;
* finite-width-specific signed corrections;
* nonlinear estimators.

Those are not counterexamples to the theorem; they are outside its claim.
