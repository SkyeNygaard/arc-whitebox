## 2. `Pasted markdown(9).md` / article framing

### Retire

> The remaining gap is not primarily computational or geometric; it is an observability gap.

This is too universal because several legal corrections have real average alignment and because the tested failures do not close all observables.

### Replace with

> The remaining gap is an **observability-and-deployment gap for the tested information classes**. Several legal corrections carry real signed signal, but none simultaneously achieves stable phase, sufficient oracle capture, safe tails, and favorable compute. New observables or representations remain open.

### Retire

> Future work requires a qualitatively new source of absolute phase—not another transformation of the same cloud.

### Replace with

> Reopening a closed branch requires either a genuinely new information source or a representation that extracts downstream signed phase not captured by the tested same-cloud summaries. Transformations of the same cloud are not ruled out universally, but repeating the frozen feature dictionaries is not justified.

### Remove scalar threshold claims

Do not present approximately 0.3%, 0.45%, or \(5\times10^{-4}\) as universal layer-31 gates. Replace with the downstream-weighted exact-replay criterion and label any numerical threshold as direction-, cohort-, and replay-specific.
