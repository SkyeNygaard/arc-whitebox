# Decision

## Overall verdict

# THE PROPOSED OBSERVABILITY-GAP IMPOSSIBILITY THEOREM IS INVALID AS FORMULATED

The blueprint's central C3/C4 route assumes a Gaussian random-field prior for the observed output. The retained challenge implementation applies ReLU after the final matrix multiplication, so the output field is a nonlinear transform of any Gaussian wide-network preactivation and is not Gaussian. Classical Gaussian average-case Bayes-linearity/no-adaptation theorems therefore do not establish the claimed result.

## Surviving theorem

The complete-support uniform mass-one rule is exactly optimal among fixed linear weights at every width by zonal symmetry and constant Kerdock Gram row sums. If total mass is unconstrained, the optimum is alpha-scaled uniform; at infinite width `alpha*=0.9999997503247287`, an operationally negligible effect.

## Empirical decision

- Close the retained T4 target-free phase-prediction dictionary.
- Retain the 12-network transfer diagnostic as evidence against a large tested amplification mode, not a theorem.
- Retain the oracle-depth ladder as a mechanism result; C7 remains conditional because coherence/cross-terms are missing.
- Treat several companion/radial corrections as real-signal but nondeployable due tails, cost, or incomplete oracle capture.
- Do not claim `gamma(256)` is bounded by failed learners.
- Retain TEST-7 as rigorous but quantitatively weak.

## Publication decision

Proceed with a scoped static-linear theorem and empirical falsification-map paper. Remove C3, C4, C8, and the claimed observability-gap conclusion.
