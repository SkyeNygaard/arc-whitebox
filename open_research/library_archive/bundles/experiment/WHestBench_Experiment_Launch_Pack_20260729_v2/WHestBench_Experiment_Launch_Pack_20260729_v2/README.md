# WHestBench Experiment Launch Pack v2

This package reconciles the full-depth adjoint/source and sparse radial-Hermite continuation into the canonical research state.

## Central update

- Tensor-free adjoint contraction is exact and terminal c21 geometry is highly compressible.
- The useful source is distributed across roughly 19 effective layers; ordinary short-suffix regeneration is no longer the default path.
- A sparse radial-Hermite control has a reconstructed exact-anchor candidate/base ratio of 0.1093 on eight exposed development networks.
- The complete anchor requires joint lower-order mean/covariance and connected-cubic functionals. Connected c21 alone is harmful in the tested sparse control.
- One-basis, fold-crossfit, Gaussian and tested joint deployable anchors are closed.
- The leading new experiment is sparse radial-Hermite layer-31 residual control:
  `E[g31] + Q_K(a31 - g31)`, followed by one real final-layer replay.
