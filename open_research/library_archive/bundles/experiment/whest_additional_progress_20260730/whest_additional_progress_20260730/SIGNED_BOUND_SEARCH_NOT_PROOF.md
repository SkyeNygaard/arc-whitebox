# Search versus proof boundary for the signed-node floor

The harmonic subset search and linear program are discovery tools only. They are not part of the theorem trust base.

The proof artifact freezes eleven feature spaces and rationally rounded objective contributions. It then:

1. reconstructs the interval Taylor jet of the ReLU kernel;
2. projects it to rigorous lower bounds for Gegenbauer coefficients through degree 26;
3. computes every squared reproducing-kernel coefficient exactly with SymPy rationals;
4. verifies every coefficient-budget inequality with Python `Fraction` arithmetic;
5. verifies every rank floor exactly;
6. sums the exact feasible contributions.

Therefore a solver bug could at worst prevent discovery of a stronger combination; it cannot invalidate the released lower bound.
