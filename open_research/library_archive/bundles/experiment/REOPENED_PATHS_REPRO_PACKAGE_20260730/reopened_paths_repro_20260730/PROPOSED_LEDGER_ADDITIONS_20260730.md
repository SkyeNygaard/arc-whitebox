# Proposed canonical-ledger additions

## Downstream-weighted archived-anchor rescore
- **Evidence:** exposed archived full-width records; 52 cases, 1,092 arm-records.
- **Result:** best fixed external arm `0.852×` pooled raw and `0.844×` cross, but worst `1.924×` and rotation-expansion `1.078×`.
- **Verdict:** retain corrected evaluation metric; no deployable revival.

## Network-derived Poisson controls
- **Evidence:** 4 fit + 16 exploratory + 16 preregistered terminal width-256 synthetic networks.
- **Result:** exploratory `0.929×`; terminal `1.038×`, 7/16 wins.
- **Verdict:** tested dictionary closed.

## Exact-mean projected-ReLU controls
- **Evidence:** 4 fit + 16 exploratory + 48 preregistered terminal width-256 synthetic networks.
- **Result:** final `1.013×` raw, `1.016×` cross, bootstrap 95% `[0.959,1.062]`; favorable adjusted `1.067×`.
- **Verdict:** tested family closed.

## Outside-Kerdock signed near-collision probes
- **Evidence:** 4 fit + 64 frozen validation width-256 synthetic networks.
- **Result:** network-derived oracle `0.131×`, but frozen global `1.557×`, 8/64 wins, worst `5.404×`.
- **Verdict:** oracle/phase benchmark retained; transferable global-weight family closed.
