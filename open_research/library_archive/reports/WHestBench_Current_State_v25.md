## Executive state

The project has made substantial mathematical progress but no competitive estimator progress.

Three distinct frontiers must remain separate:

1. **Strongest unconditional static theorem — T74**  
   Every static, network-independent, mass-one signed rule in the declared limiting-kernel class has risk at least `0.9370605225569535` of Kerdock risk. The maximum same-cost improvement is `1.0671669288460727x`.

2. **Strongest conditional static theorem candidate — T80**  
   The Infinite Hermite factor would raise the floor to `0.9546250809178666` and cap same-cost improvement at `1.0475316645132617x`, but only after proving one all-degree coefficient-positivity lemma and interval-certifying the three contact roots. This is not yet a theorem.

3. **Only plausible competition mechanism — T70–T73**  
   Construct a physical network-covariant rank-4/5 correction at the final hidden checkpoint, estimate only its few scalar contractions, and replay the final layer exactly. This remains open because no source has simultaneously demonstrated winning oracle capacity, legal observability, and favorable variance-cost economics.

## Best proof paths

### Infinite Hermite factor

This is now the most concrete static proof project. The finite degree-63 through degree-123 certificates appear to be truncations of one analytic factor `L` satisfying `L^2=K32-q`, where `q` is a degree-five three-contact Hermite interpolant.

The missing proof is narrow:

- interval-isolate the roots and branch;
- certify a finite Taylor prefix with Arb;
- prove the remaining coefficients positive using explicit endpoint Puiseux/Darboux remainder bounds;
- recompute the final floor with directed intervals.

### Width-256 theorem

T76 gives the valid architecture: finite-width kernels are nonnegative Gaussian-chaos moment sequences, and harmonic coefficients must be bounded through a moment primal/dual with an infinite-tail certificate. Finite point samples and qualitative kernel convergence are insufficient.

Begin at degree 62, then 84, then 128.

### Atomic realizability

T61–T64, T74, and T78 show that abstract block matrices are sharp but actual spherical atomic rules face strictness, conditioning, inertia, and sign-count restrictions. A second static proof route is a quantitative low-degree sphere-ideal/localizing or catalecticant separation.
