## Reopening condition

M195 should be reconsidered only after another branch supplies a frozen analytic estimator whose expectation is legally and economically computable and whose residual is materially smaller under the **complete-block** metric. At that point, the required source-space and output-space \(R^2\) must be recalculated from the new anchor’s exact variance reduction, action on final MSE and full QERNO inference cost.

Until then, constructing the neural operator would be an expensive attempt to learn nearly the full integral from a limited number of independent networks—the exact situation Prompt 8 was written to avoid.
