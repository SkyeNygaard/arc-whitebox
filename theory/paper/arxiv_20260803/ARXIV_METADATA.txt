TITLE
Limits of Static Cubature for Deep ReLU Gaussian Expectations

AUTHOR
Skye Nygaard

AFFILIATION
Independent Researcher

PRIMARY CATEGORY
cs.LG

SUGGESTED CROSS-LIST
math.NA

COMMENTS
9 pages. Computer-assisted bounds for static, network-independent linear cubature at a fixed node budget. Proof certificates and verification scripts are provided in the accompanying artifact repository.

ABSTRACT
Gaussian expectations of neural networks appear in uncertainty propagation, model analysis, and deterministic inference. When each network evaluation is expensive, a natural approach is to replace Monte Carlo samples with a fixed weighted rule. This paper studies the best possible performance of such rules for the infinite-width kernel of a dimension-256, depth-32 bias-free ReLU network.

Positive homogeneity reduces the Gaussian expectation to integration on the unit sphere. A complete system of 129 real mutually unbiased bases, generated by Kerdock structure and evaluated antipodally, gives a rule with 66,048 nodes. We prove that this rule is at most 0.0233242% above the optimum among all nonnegative mass-one rules with the same node budget. We also prove that arbitrary signed mass-one rules can reduce its kernel risk by at most 6.2940%. The first bound follows from an all-degree Delsarte certificate with a unique degree-five Hermite optimizer. The second uses harmonic moment constraints, matrix inertia, and an exact-rational witness. Together, the results show that fixed network-independent linear cubature is nearly exhausted for this kernel and budget.
